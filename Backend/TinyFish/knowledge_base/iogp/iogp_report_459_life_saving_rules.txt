# IOGP REPORT 459: LIFE-SAVING RULES (INTERNATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF OIL & GAS PRODUCERS)

## 1. CONFINED SPACE ENTRY
Rule: Obtain authorization before entering a confined space.
- Confirm energy sources are isolated.
- Confirm the atmosphere has been tested for oxygen deficiency, flammable vapours (LEL), and toxic gases (H2S, CO) and is confirmed safe before entry.
- Continuously monitor the atmosphere if required by the risk assessment.
- Check and use your breathing apparatus and personal gas detector when required.
- Confirm that a qualified Standby Person (Hole Watch) is stationed outside the entrance at all times during occupancy.
- Confirm a rescue plan and emergency retrieval equipment are in place and operational.

## 2. ENERGY ISOLATION (LOTO)
Rule: Verify isolation before work begins and use the specified life-protecting equipment.
- Confirm all energy sources (electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, stored pressure, chemical) have been de-energized.
- Apply personal Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) padlocks and tags at designated isolation points.
- Verify zero energy state through testing (voltage check, pressure gauge bleed-off, drain verification).
- Never remove another worker's lock or bypass an isolation barrier without formal authorization.

## 3. HOT WORK
Rule: Control flammables and ignition sources.
- Identify and control all flammable and combustible materials within a 15-metre radius.
- Conduct continuous atmospheric gas testing for Lower Explosive Limit (LEL) prior to and during hot work.
- Maintain a dedicated, qualified Fire Watch with charged fire extinguisher during the work and for at least 30 minutes following completion.
- Obtain an authorized Hot Work Permit before ignition.

## 4. WORK AT HEIGHT
Rule: Protect yourself against a fall when working at height.
- Inspect fall protection equipment (full-body harness, lanyards, carabiners, deceleration devices) prior to use.
- Maintain 100% tie-off to an approved anchor point rated for at least 22 kN (5,000 lbs).
- Verify scaffolding is tagged GREEN and has intact guardrails, mid-rails, and toe-boards.
- Use fall arrest or restraint systems when working above 1.8 metres (6 feet) where passive collective protection is absent.

## 5. LINE OF FIRE
Rule: Keep yourself and others out of the line of fire.
- Position yourself to avoid moving equipment, pressurized fluid streams, high-pressure test lines, and projectile trajectories.
- Never stand or walk beneath a suspended load or within the swing radius of lifting equipment.
- Verify exclusion zones are demarcated with physical barriers and warning signage.
- Establish eye contact and clear hand/radio signals with operators (cranes, winch trucks, rig top drives) before entering work zones.

## 6. BYPASSING SAFETY CONTROLS
Rule: Obtain authorization before overriding or disabling safety controls.
- Understand and never bypass critical safety instrumented systems (SIS), Emergency Shutdown (ESD) valves, pressure safety relief valves (PSV), or gas detection sensors without formal Management of Change (MOC) and bypass permit.
- Implement documented compensatory measures and heightened supervision during any approved bypass.
- Restore bypassed devices to service and conduct functional re-testing prior to normal operation.

## 7. MECHANICAL LIFTING
Rule: Plan lifting operations and control the area.
- Confirm the lifting equipment, cranes, rigging slings, shackles, and wire ropes are certified, undamaged, and within valid color-code inspection periods.
- Verify the total load weight is within rated safe working load (SWL) capacity per the approved lifting plan.
- Demarcate the lift path and drop exclusion zone; station a designated banksman/rigger to direct operations.

## 8. GROUND DISTURBANCE
Rule: Stop and verify underground services before digging or excavating.
- Obtain an authorized Ground Disturbance Permit.
- Conduct electronic utility locating surveys and review updated pipeline and cable route drawings.
- Hand-dig exploratory inspection pits (potholing) within 1 metre of marked underground pipelines and cables.

## 9. DRIVING SAFETY
Rule: Follow safe driving rules and journey management plans.
- Always wear seatbelts; adhere to designated site speed limits (maximum 20 km/h on internal oilfield access tracks).
- Never use mobile phones (including hands-free) while driving.
- Verify driver fatigue limits, pre-trip vehicle roadworthiness checklist, and journey management clearance.
