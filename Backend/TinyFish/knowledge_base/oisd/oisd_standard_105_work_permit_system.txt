# OISD STANDARD 105: WORK PERMIT SYSTEM (OIL INDUSTRY SAFETY DIRECTORATE, INDIA)

## CLAUSE 5.1: CONTROL OF WORK AND PERMIT CATEGORIES
- A Work Permit (PTW) is a formal written document issued by an authorized custodian to a performing authority authorizing specified maintenance or inspection activities within a designated hydrocarbon-processing facility.
- Mandatory categories include: Hot Work Permit (Class A / Class B), Cold Work Permit, Confined Space Entry Permit, Electrical Isolation / Energization Permit, and Excavation Permit.

## CLAUSE 5.3: CONFINED SPACE ENTRY REQUIREMENTS
1. Atmospheric Testing Standards:
   - Prior to any entry into a tank, separator vessel, column, pit, or manhole, mandatory atmospheric testing must be performed by an Authorized Gas Tester (AGT).
   - Oxygen concentration must be verified strictly between 19.5% and 23.5% by volume.
   - Hydrocarbon flammable vapor concentration must be 0% LEL (Lower Explosive Limit) for entry without breathing apparatus, and strictly below 10% LEL with air-supplied respiratory equipment.
   - Toxic gases must be tested: Hydrogen Sulfide (H2S) must be below 10 ppm permissible exposure limit (PEL). If H2S exceeds 10 ppm, entry is prohibited without positive-pressure SCBA.
2. Stationing of Standby Person (Hole Watch):
   - A trained, dedicated Standby Person must be stationed outside the entrance hatch or manhole at all times while personnel remain inside.
   - The Standby Person must maintain continuous visual or verbal communication with all entrants.
   - The Standby Person must never abandon the manhole post or attempt internal rescue without first raising the alarm and awaiting the certified emergency rescue team.
3. Personal Safety Equipment and Retrieval:
   - Entrants must wear safety harnesses connected to lifelines with tripod recovery hoists erected over vertical manways where practical.
   - Continuous forced extraction or air-blowing ventilation must be maintained throughout the entry period.

## CLAUSE 5.5: POSITIVE ENERGY ISOLATION & LOTO
1. Mechanical Isolation:
   - Hydrocarbon pipelines connected to equipment undergoing internal work must be positively isolated by physical blind flanges, spectacle blinds, or double-block-and-bleed valve arrangements with vent bleeders locked open.
2. Electrical Isolation:
   - Electrical switchgear and circuit breakers driving associated pumps, mixers, or heaters must be tagged out and padlocked (LOTO) by an authorized electrical engineer, followed by local push-button attempt-to-start verification.
